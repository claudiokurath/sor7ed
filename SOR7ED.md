# SOR7ED — Master Document

## What it is

SOR7ED (pronounced "sorted") is a diagnostic tool for people who feel overwhelmed, scattered, or stuck. You answer 4 questions, get a personalised result, and send it to WhatsApp in one tap. No app download. No login required. Under 2 minutes.

WhatsApp is not just the delivery mechanism — it is the product. The website diagnoses. WhatsApp is the dashboard.

## The problem it solves

Most people know something is wrong. They don't know exactly what, or what to do first. SOR7ED identifies the specific friction point and gives them the exact next steps — delivered where they already live: WhatsApp.

## How it works

1. User lands on the site or messages the WhatsApp number directly
2. They pick a tool that matches their situation
3. They answer 4 targeted questions
4. They get a personalised result — a specific friction type, not a generic label
5. They tap one button to send their result to WhatsApp
6. They receive a step-by-step protocol on WhatsApp

Users can also text anything directly — "can't sleep", "too much to do", "burnt out" — and get routed to the right tool automatically.

## WhatsApp as the dashboard

WhatsApp is the core interface. The web app is the entry point. Key features:

- **Save to WhatsApp** button on every tool and article — saves the content directly to the user's WhatsApp for later
- **Rich links** — content shared via WhatsApp renders with full previews
- **Protocol delivery** — full step-by-step protocols sent as WhatsApp messages
- **Keyword routing** — text any keyword and get matched to the right tool
- **Weekly broadcasts** — opt-in weekly check-ins, cancellable with STOPWEEKLY
- **Crisis detection** — flagged messages handled separately
- **STOP compliance** — full opt-out at any time

## The tools

Each tool is an assessment. Users answer 4 questions and get one of several possible results. Each result has a friction type, a headline, a subheadline, an insight, and 2 protocol steps. The full protocol arrives on WhatsApp.

Current tools:
- Money Reset (4 Accounts)
- Meltdown First Aid
- Memory Palace Builder
- Meeting Translator
- Financial Autopilot
- Body Double Matchmaker
- Sensory Audit

## The articles

Articles (called "Field Reads") explain the why behind each friction type — what's actually happening, not just what it feels like. Each article links to the relevant tool.

## The content model

All content lives in Notion and syncs to Supabase via a cron job. Only content marked Published in Notion appears on the site.

## The account system

Users can create a free account to save assessment history and track progress. It is optional — tools work fully without one.

## Who it's for

Anyone who feels overwhelmed, scattered, or stuck. Built using neuro-inclusive principles, which means it works especially well for people with ADHD or autism — and for everyone else too.

## What it is not

- Not therapy
- Not a clinical diagnosis service
- Not a subscription app
- Not a generic productivity tool
