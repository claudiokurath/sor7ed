## Executive Summary
This doc is the single source of truth for building the SOR7ED platform. It outlines the overall vision, product definition, technical architecture, and current development goals.

---

## Goals for this Build
- Build a fast, reliable website that pulls content from Notion.
- Make WhatsApp delivery feel instant, safe, and consistent.
- Enforce GDPR/PECR consent, crisis overrides, and STOP unsubscribes.
- Support a clear free → paid conversion path (subscriptions and credit packs).

---

## Product Definition
### What SOR7ED Is
- A content site (public) + member area (private).
- A WhatsApp‑first delivery system for keyword‑triggered templates.
- A Notion‑powered CMS and single source of truth.

### What SOR7ED Is **Not**
- Not therapy.
- Not medical advice.
- Not a crisis service.

## Technical Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js (App Router) v16.2.6
- **Auth & Database:** Supabase (Auth, DB)
- **CMS:** Notion API
- **Hosting:** Vercel
- **Languages:** TypeScript, React 19, TailwindCSS
